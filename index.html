How this app works

  1. Add a trade when you finish one. Enter the coin, how many you traded, the price you bought at and the price you sold at.
  2. Add expenses like exchange fees, tax or internet. They are subtracted from your profit.
  3. Open Summary any time to see your profit, loss and net profit for today, any day or any month.

Simple words

Profit
Money you made on winning trades.
Loss
Money you lost on losing trades.
Trading profit / loss
Profit minus loss, after trade fees.
Expenses
Other money you spent, like tax or internet.
Net profit
What you really kept: trading profit / loss minus expenses.

Install on your phone

Android: in Chrome tap ⋮ then Install app.
iPhone: in Safari tap Share then Add to Home Screen.

Forgot your password?

On the login screen tap Forgot password?. Enter your registered phone number or email, verify the code shown in this device’s notification, then choose a new password. Your encrypted trades stay intact.

Lock

The app locks by itself after 5 minutes.
